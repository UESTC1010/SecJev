# LANL authentication / redteam data

Source: https://csr.lanl.gov/data/cyber1/
License: CC0 1.0, as stated on the official source page saved in metadata/lanl-source-page.html.
Citation: Alexander D. Kent, Comprehensive, Multi-Source Cyber-Security Events, Los Alamos National Laboratory, 2015. DOI: 10.17021/1179829.
Related publication: A. D. Kent, Cybersecurity Data Sources for Dynamic Network Research, in Dynamic Networks in Cybersecurity, 2015.
The original source warns that failed authentications are included only for users with at least one successful authentication somewhere in the dataset. This creates selection bias.
No registration credentials or private transport logs are included in this release.
